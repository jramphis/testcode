# Maternity Without Walls: verification pass notes

Pass run 13–14 September 2026 against the thirteen open items in Section 10 of the
13 September 2026 decision draft, plus the one claim the draft cited from general
knowledge (Dutch kraamzorg) and the post-cutoff technology claims in Section 7.

## Method and its limit

- Four parallel research agents, one per theme (payment, workforce and legal,
  epidemiology and facilities, federal programs and device registries).
- The research environment's egress policy blocked page fetches to every primary
  host tried (ASES/docs.pr.gov, Medicaid.gov, CMS, KFF, MACPAC, Congress.gov,
  LexJuris, SUTRA, CDC/NCHS, salud.pr.gov, FDA, USASpending, HRSA, PubMed,
  Federal Register, and all Puerto Rico press). The documented APIs were tried
  (USASpending POST, api.fda.gov, E-utilities, api.federalregister.gov) and were
  blocked at the CONNECT layer, not by request shape.
- Web search worked until the session budget (200 queries) was exhausted. Every
  finding is therefore corroborated from indexed text of the primary source, not
  read from the document. The paper says so in Section 10 and in Appendix B.
- The federal-programs agent (Title V, Healthy Start, MIECHV, ERASE MM, PRAMS,
  FDA device records, Part 108, E-MOTIVE PMIDs, TMaH, FQHC UDS) got one query in
  before the budget lapsed. Those items remain open and are assigned in
  Section 10.2.
